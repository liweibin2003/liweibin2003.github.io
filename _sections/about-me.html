--- title: About Me icon: fa-user order: 2 ---

My journey into the digital world began not in a classroom, but in video games. Back in 2013, I started publishing gaming videos on Chinese platforms. By 2018, I experienced the thrill of creator monetization firsthand, earning RMB 2,000 in a single week. But I wanted to be more than just a player—I wanted to be a builder. I taught myself to code and launched my own Minecraft server, growing it into a thriving community of over 100 players.

At university, I majored in Communication, where I developed a strong foundation in photography and visual storytelling. Yet, my inner "geek" never faded. I've always been obsessed with how emerging technologies can redefine creative workflows. I was an early adopter of Stable Diffusion in 2022, and during my Global Media Buying internship at Mobvista in 2025, I successfully bridged my two passions: I introduced Python programming into my team's day-to-day operations, engineering 15 automation scripts that drastically scaled our productivity.
My fascination with the business side of the digital world was sparked in 2023 when an executive at PwC introduced me to growth marketing. It was a lightbulb moment. I realized that the most impactful work happens where technology, creative content, and data-driven insights intersect.
This realization guided my next big academic decision. While I received offers from HKU and UCL for traditional Communication programs, I deliberately chose to pursue an MA in Digital Sociology with Social Data Science at the University of Edinburgh. For me, this wasn't just a degree choice—it was a strategic pivot to understand people, creator communities, and social commerce through the rigorous lens of data and algorithms.
I've been a creator, a community manager, and a data-driven marketer. Now, I'm excited to keep exploring this intersection and leverage my unique blend of empathy for creators and analytical rigor to shape the future of social commerce.
