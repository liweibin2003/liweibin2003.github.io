--- title: About Me icon: fa-user order: 2 ---

I'm a 22-year-old from China with a longstanding interest in technology, creativity, and the ways they intersect. My journey began with video games. In 2013, I started publishing gaming videos on Chinese platforms, and by 2018, I was earning RMB 2,000 in a single week from my videos. That same year, I wanted to go beyond simply playing games, so I taught myself to code and built my own Minecraft server. Over the following months, it grew into a community that attracted more than 100 players. At university, I chose to study Communication, where I discovered a passion for photography and visual storytelling. At the same time, I continued to follow emerging technologies closely. In 2022, I was among the early adopters of Stable Diffusion. Later, during my internship at Mobvista in 2025, I brought programming into my team's day-to-day workflow for the first time, exploring how technical skills could improve the way we work. My interests have always spanned both the creative and analytical worlds, from visual expression and storytelling to programming and data analysis. This combination has shaped the way I approach problems and opened up a wide range of possibilities for my future career. In 2023, Paul, an executive at PwC, first introduced me to digital marketing as a potential career path. The field has continued to fascinate me ever since, particularly because of its potential to bring together technology, creativity, and data-driven insights. When choosing my next academic step, I received offers from the University of Hong Kong and UCL for Communication-related programmes, but ultimately chose to pursue Digital Sociology with Social Data Science at the University of Edinburgh. For me, this represents a natural continuation of my interests: understanding people and society through the lens of data, technology, and digital culture. I'm excited to keep exploring the intersections of these fields and to see where this combination of interests will take me next.
